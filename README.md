# 🌙 Gardens of Noor

### A peaceful board game for children ages 6–12

Gardens of Noor is a web-based board game where kids learn kindness, honesty, patience, helping others, and sharing — through gentle, engaging gameplay instead of lectures.

---

## ✨ Features

- 🎲 *Roll & Move Gameplay* — simple, intuitive mechanics designed for young children
- 🌱 *Values-Based Learning* — each space and event reinforces a core Islamic value (kindness, honesty, patience, sharing, helping others)
- 🎨 *Calm, Child-Friendly Design* — a peaceful garden theme instead of competitive or aggressive visuals
- 👨‍👩‍👧 *Family & Classroom Friendly* — designed to be played together, at home or in an academy setting

## 🎯 Why I Built This

As the founder of Al Quran Online Academy, I wanted a way for children to experience good character, not just hear about it. Gardens of Noor turns values education into something kids look forward to — play that shapes character.
